# Review criteria

- The headline, primary action, and actual purpose are immediately clear.
- Manuscript/transcript visual material carries the mechanism; illustrative content is labeled.
- No overlap, clipping, horizontal overflow, or inaccessible controls at 390, 704, or 1440px.
- Source Sans 3 is self-hosted; ink/blue palette and Phosphor icons are consistent.
- Motion has a processing/storytelling purpose and respects reduced motion.
- Studio controls perform real work: upload, preparation, settings, processing, edit/save, region correction, and export.
- Model readiness, raw model scores, partial failure, and reference evaluation are honest.
- Visual reviewer judges screenshots and the supplied implementation, with no claim that a screenshot proves backend recognition accuracy.
